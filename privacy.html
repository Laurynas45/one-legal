Privacy Policy (English, for hosting)

Last updated: 16 September 2026.

This is the English source for a public HTTPS page. The same content ships in-app (EN/ES/ZH) under Welcome or Me → Privacy Policy. English is official.

**ONE is not yet published on the App Store or Google Play.**

Operator: Placebo Drinks Ltd (confirm the legal entity name in App Store Connect / Play Console before launch). App identifier: `com.placebodrinks.one`.

Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the ONE mobile app (“ONE”, “we”) handles information. ONE is a GPS-based social, dating, and marketplace product.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use ONE we process: account details (username, display name; this MVP stores a password only on-device), date of birth and region for the age gate, contact channel (email or phone) for two-tier verification, approximate or precise location when you grant GPS permission, profile content (bio, skills, hobbies, services, website links, gender icon, visibility), photos you upload for identity checks or the private gallery, social graph (friends, location-sharing choices), user-generated posts, comments, and marketplace listings, optional advertising campaign drafts, language preference, and reports/blocks you submit.

We do not collect contacts from your address book, payment cards, or bank details.

Location sharing

ONE uses GPS to place you on a map of nearby verified members. Location is used for app functionality, not for selling location data. You can appear visible or incognito. Incognito hides you from everyone except accepted friends you explicitly share location with. You can revoke system location permission in iOS/Android settings. Background location is not requested.

Dating and social features

Profiles, matching, the feed, and friend requests are social features. Other members may see information you choose to make visible. Matching uses interests and complementary services you add to your profile.

Age verification

ONE is an 18+ (19+ in the South Korea placeholder) dating, marketplace, and social product. We collect date of birth and region solely to enforce the age gate. Store listings should be rated 17+ (mature) because the app includes dating and user-generated content. Underage users are not permitted.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access are used for identity verification and for profile or private-gallery uploads. Nudity is not allowed. Other speech and imagery from verified members is allowed. Photos stay on-device in this MVP. A future KYC vendor would process identity images under its own terms, disclosed before enabling.

Content policy

ONE is a free-speech app. Verified members may post anything except nudity on public profiles, the private gallery, and the feed — that is the only hard content ban on those surfaces. We also prohibit illegal content that Apple and Google require us to remove, such as child sexual abuse material and credible threats of violence. Report and Block are member safety tools, not general speech moderation.

Advertising

Advertising is the only intended paid feature. Checkout is not live. We do not use your data for third-party advertising tracking in this version, and we do not sell personal information.

Account deletion and retention

Delete your account in Me → Delete account. This removes your profile and associated content from this device. When a production backend exists, deletion will also be processed on the server. Signing out does not delete your account.

Your choices and rights

You can change visibility, stop sharing location with a friend, revoke OS permissions, switch language (English, Spanish, Chinese), and delete your account. Depending on your region you may have access, correction, and deletion rights. Contact the operator via the in-app support assistant until a public privacy email is published.

Children

ONE is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as ONE adds a real backend, KYC vendor, or payments. Last updated 16 September 2026.
